# Security policy

PlainTx protects people's funds, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@plaintx.app** or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository.
Do not open a public issue.

Please include what you found, steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect. We aim to acknowledge
reports within 72 hours.

## In scope

- A verdict that misses a known-dangerous transaction, signature or approval ("false all-clear").
- Anything that could lead a user to sign something harmful.
- Leaks of secrets or user data.

## Never

PlainTx will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is not us.
